SELL ON MONOLITH

Open your $XRP storefront.

Your MONOLITH coordinate becomes your verified storefront. Sign in with the wallet that owns the coordinate, create your seller profile, complete verification, then start publishing physical merchandise.

MONOLITH Merch Fee: 3%. MONOLITH deducts 3% from merchandise sales. Shipping is not charged a marketplace fee. Buyers make one $XRP payment to MONOLITH, and your seller proceeds are paid out after payment validation.

By selling through MONOLITH, you agree to the Merch Store Rules and understand that products and seller accounts may be reviewed, restricted, suspended, or removed for policy violations, legal concerns, fraud, safety issues, or marketplace abuse.
