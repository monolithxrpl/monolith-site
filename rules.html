MONOLITH Rules

MONOLITH is XRPL coordinate real estate. A tile is a fixed public coordinate tied to a wallet, a tag, and optional public details.

1. What You Are Buying

You are buying a permanent public tile position on MONOLITH.

  • Your tile has a coordinate. Example formats only: N12E8, S4W1, or N589.
  • Your tile can show a tag, wallet, handle, link, note, and image.
  • Your tile becomes part of the public wall record after verified ownership activation.
  • MONOLITH does not hold your wallet keys and does not take custody of your assets.

2. Wallet Ownership

  • One wallet can own unlimited tiles.
  • One tile has one owner wallet.
  • Owner wallet is immutable after ownership activation.
  • Tile owners can edit tag, handle, link, note, and image through Xaman owner SignIn.
  • Editing requires the current owner wallet to sign. MONOLITH does not use passwords or custody.

3. Reserved Coordinates and Availability

  • Reserved coordinates are controlled availability positions.
  • Reserved does not mean free.
  • Some coordinates may display reserved imagery before purchase.
  • Visual reserved imagery does not remove the payment requirement unless MONOLITH activates ownership through an official process.
  • Availability is controlled by backend ownership and reservation status.

4. Coordinate Format

The public format is simple: use north or south first, then east or west.

  • Main format examples only: N12E8, S4W1, N8W24.
  • Axis-only format: N589, S4, E10, W24.
  • Raw coordinate format also works: -24,8.
  • Dashes and lowercase are cleaned automatically.

5. Pricing

Prices are shown in USD. Payment is made in $XRP through Xaman at checkout.

Tier Price What It Means
Regular $99 USD Standard claimable coordinates.

The backend converts the USD price into a live $XRP quote at checkout. Quotes expire after 5 minutes.

6. Signature Numbers

Some numbers are more valuable because people recognize them, want them, or use them as symbols.

  • Axis-only tiles like N1, S2, E10, and W24 are Signature tier.
  • Numbers like 42, 67, 69, 86, 143, 314, 404, 420, 589, 777, 808, 888, 911, 999, 1337, 1776, 1978, 2012, and 2026 are Signature tier.
  • Repeating numbers like 111, 222, 333, 444, 555, 777, 888, and 999 are Signature tier.

7. Premium Numbers

Some numbers are stronger than regular coordinates but not Signature.

  • Examples: 10, 11, 22, 25, 33, 44, 50, 55, 66, 75, 77, 99, 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500.
  • Mirrored paired coordinates where both axes use the same number, such as N113E113 or S250W250, are Premium tier unless blocked, claimed, reserved, axis-only, or upgraded by Signature rules.
  • If a number appears in both Premium and Signature rules, Signature wins.
  • If a number is blocked, blocked wins over every tier.

8. Not Available

Some coordinates are not for sale.

  • Any coordinate with 666 on either axis is not available.
  • Examples: N666, S666, E666, W666, N666E1, and N1E666.
  • Blocked coordinates override every price tier.

9. Reserved Tiles

Reserved means the tile is being held. It does not mean free.

  • Reserved coordinates still show their real price unless MONOLITH activates ownership through an official process.
  • A reserved Regular tile is still $99 USD.
  • A reserved Premium tile is still $249 USD.
  • A reserved Signature tile is still $499 USD.
  • Reserved tiles are held and released only when MONOLITH chooses to release them.

10. Abuse Enforcement

Normal purchases do not require approval. If a buyer abuses the wall, MONOLITH may refund the purchase, lock the tile, and block the wallet from future claims.

  • No approval lane for normal purchases.
  • Abuse is handled after purchase through refund, tile lock, and wallet block.
  • Unavailable, claimed, backend-reserved, or blocked tiles cannot be purchased. Visual RESERVED image placeholders can still be purchased unless backend status blocks them.

11. Claim Requirements

  • Use a valid coordinate.
  • Use a valid XRPL classic wallet address starting with r.
  • Tag is optional: 1 to 18 characters, letters, numbers, and underscores only.
  • Link is optional: use an @handle or full https URL.
  • Note is optional: max 160 characters, no angle brackets.
  • Image is optional: PNG, JPG, or WEBP only, max 2 MB.

12. Launch Flow

The claim page is live. It creates live quotes, Xaman payment requests, backend-verified ownership records, and Coordinate Deed accept links.

  • Checkout runs through Xaman.
  • Payment will be made in $XRP.
  • The backend verifies the XRPL payment.
  • The tile will update only after backend verification.
  • The wall refreshes tile panels and visible tile images from backend ownership data when available.

13. Final Rules

  • No scams.
  • No illegal content.
  • No impersonation.
  • No malicious links.
  • No abusive images.
  • No fake wallet claims.
  • MONOLITH can reject, reserve, or block coordinates that create risk for the wall.

Tile Abuse, Abuse Enforcement, and Reporting

Tile Abuse

MONOLITH is built for real coordinate ownership, identity, expression, and public presence. Clean multi-tile ownership is allowed. Abuse is not protected.

MONOLITH reserves the right to review, hide, lock, remove, refund, restrict, or block any tile, wallet, link, image, profile, claim, or related content that abuses the platform, misleads users, damages the integrity of the wall, or violates these rules.

Examples of Abuse

  • Scams, phishing, malware, fake claim pages, wallet drainers, or malicious links.
  • Impersonation of people, projects, companies, brands, public figures, or communities.
  • Illegal content or content used to support illegal activity.
  • Harassment, threats, doxxing, targeted abuse, or intimidation.
  • Spam, misleading links, deceptive redirects, or low-quality abuse of tile pages.
  • Trademark, logo, brand, or identity abuse.
  • Pornographic, graphic, shock, or inappropriate content.
  • Hate, extremist, or violent promotional content.
  • Visual wall abuse intended to deface, manipulate, dominate, or hijack the wall experience.

Block Clusters and Multi-Tile Images

Buying or controlling multiple tiles is allowed. MONOLITH is coordinate real estate, and one wallet may own multiple clean tiles.

Legitimate multi-tile murals are allowed when one image is intentionally split across adjacent tiles to form one larger square or rectangular image, as long as the mural is clean, non-abusive, non-spammy, and not used to scam, impersonate, harass, deface, manipulate, or visually hijack the wall.

Abusive block clusters are not allowed. This includes groups of adjacent tiles using the same image, repeated image, tiled image, copy-paste image, or coordinated visual pattern to spam, dominate, deface, manipulate, mislead, impersonate, or visually hijack the wall.

Report a Tile

If you believe a tile is being used for abuse, scams, phishing, impersonation, inappropriate content, spam, harassment, misleading links, visual wall abuse, abusive block clustering, or any other violation, report it for review.

Use the report form: Report a MONOLITH tile

Email reports can also be sent to: monolith.xrpl@gmail.com

Reports should include:

  • Tile coordinate.
  • Reason for the report.
  • Short description of the issue.
  • Relevant wallet, profile link, website link, or screenshot if available.
  • Your email address if you want to be contacted. This is optional.

Reports do not automatically remove a tile. MONOLITH reviews reports manually and may take action when a tile, wallet, link, image, profile, claim, or pattern of behavior violates the rules or damages the integrity of the wall.
