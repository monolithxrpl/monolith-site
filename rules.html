MONOLITH Rules

MONOLITH is a public wall of record. A tile is a permanent coordinate tied to a public XRPL wallet, a tag, and optional public details.

1. What You Are Buying

You are buying a permanent public tile position on MONOLITH.

  • Your tile has a coordinate, like N23E11, S4W1, or N589.
  • Your tile can show a tag, wallet, handle, link, note, and image.
  • Your tile becomes part of the public wall record after launch payment verification.
  • MONOLITH does not hold your wallet keys and does not take custody of your assets.

2. Coordinate Format

The public format is simple: use north or south first, then east or west.

  • Main format: N23E11, S4W1, N8W24.
  • Axis-only format: N589, S4, E10, W24.
  • Raw coordinate format also works: -24,8.
  • Dashes and lowercase are cleaned automatically.

3. Pricing

Prices are shown in USD. Payment is made in $XRP through Xaman at checkout.

Tier Price What It Means
Regular $99 USD Standard claimable coordinates.
Premium $249 USD Cleaner numbers, repeats, milestones, and stronger positions.
Signature $499 USD Axis-only tiles, culturally significant numbers, and rare coordinates.

At launch, the backend will convert the USD price into a live $XRP quote. Quotes expire after 5 minutes.

4. Signature Numbers

Some numbers are more valuable because people recognize them, want them, or use them as symbols.

  • Axis-only tiles like N1, S2, E10, and W24 are Signature tier.
  • Numbers like 42, 67, 69, 86, 143, 314, 404, 420, 589, 777, 808, 888, 911, 999, 1337, 1776, 1978, 2012, and 2026 are Signature tier.
  • Repeating numbers like 111, 222, 333, 444, 555, 777, 888, and 999 are Signature tier.

5. Premium Numbers

Some numbers are stronger than regular coordinates but not Signature.

  • Examples: 10, 11, 22, 25, 33, 44, 50, 55, 66, 75, 77, 99, 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500.
  • If a number appears in both Premium and Signature rules, Signature wins.
  • If a number is blocked, blocked wins over every tier.

6. Not Available

Some coordinates are not for sale.

  • Any coordinate with 666 on either axis is not available.
  • Examples: N666, S666, E666, W666, N666E1, and N1E666.
  • Blocked coordinates override every price tier.

7. Reserved Tiles

Reserved means the tile is being held. It does not mean free.

  • Reserved tiles still show their real launch price.
  • A reserved Regular tile is still $99 USD.
  • A reserved Premium tile is still $249 USD.
  • A reserved Signature tile is still $499 USD.
  • Reserved tiles are held and released only when MONOLITH chooses to release them.

8. Abuse Enforcement

MONOLITH does not use a manual-review lane for normal purchases. If a buyer abuses the wall, MONOLITH may refund the purchase, lock the tile, and block the wallet from future claims.

  • No manual review tier.
  • No approval lane for normal purchases.
  • Abuse is handled after purchase through refund, tile lock, and wallet block.
  • Unavailable, claimed, reserved, or blocked tiles still cannot be purchased.

9. Claim Requirements

  • Use a valid coordinate.
  • Use a valid XRPL classic wallet address starting with r.
  • Tag is optional: 1 to 18 characters, letters, numbers, and underscores only.
  • Link is optional: use an @handle or full https URL.
  • Note is optional: max 160 characters, no angle brackets.
  • Image is optional: PNG, JPG, or WEBP only, max 2 MB.

10. Launch Flow

The current claim page is preview mode. It prepares claim data. It does not complete a real purchase yet.

  • At launch, checkout will run through Xaman.
  • Payment will be made in $XRP.
  • The backend will verify the XRPL payment.
  • The tile will update only after backend verification.
  • The wall will eventually load ownership from the API instead of static feed edits.

11. Final Rules

  • No scams.
  • No illegal content.
  • No impersonation.
  • No malicious links.
  • No abusive images.
  • No fake wallet claims.
  • MONOLITH can reject, reserve, or block coordinates that create risk for the wall.
