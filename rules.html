MONOLITH

The Wall of Record · The On-Chain Monument

Preamble

MONOLITH is a permanent public record. It exists to document proof of presence, not popularity. These rules are immutable by intent and enforced by design.

Participation in MONOLITH constitutes acceptance of all rules below.

Foundational Law

  • Immutability

    All posts on MONOLITH are permanent. No edits. No removals. No reversals.

  • Constraint

    All marks must conform to the defined tile specification. Constraint is not optional.

  • Equality

    MONOLITH follows the same rules as every participant. No privileged placement. No exceptions.

  • Finality

    Once recorded, a mark becomes part of the wall of record forever.

Identity

  • Wallet As Identity

    Public wallet address is the sole identity layer. No profiles. No usernames.

  • No Custody

    MONOLITH never holds keys, funds, or credentials.

  • Public Data Only

    All information displayed is derived from public, on-chain data.

Posting

  • Economic Friction

    Posting requires an XRP payment to prevent spam and preserve signal.

  • One Mark Per Action

    Each post represents a single, discrete act of presence.

  • Deterministic Placement

    Tile placement is algorithmic, auditable, and not manually curated.

Visibility

  • Endless Record

    The wall grows indefinitely. History is never erased.

  • Finite Attention

    Visibility is time-weighted. Newer marks surface. Older marks archive.

  • No Algorithms

    There are no likes, boosts, rankings, or engagement mechanics.

What MONOLITH Is Not

  • Not Social Media

    No comments. No followers. No feeds.

  • Not Advertising Space

    No banners. No takeovers. No promotional dominance.

  • Not Editable History

    MONOLITH does not rewrite the past.

Final Clause

MONOLITH is a credibility layer. Presence is proven by action, not noise.

If you were real back then, you’re on MONOLITH.
