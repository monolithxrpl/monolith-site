MONOLITH
XRPL public wall. Wallet-linked coordinates. Active build.
A visible layer for position, identity, and presence

MONOLITH is a public wall being built on XRPL. It connects wallets to fixed public coordinates and turns position into something visible, memorable, and permanent.

This is bigger than sending to a shorter string. Coordinates can be claimed, displayed, tied to brands, tied to projects, and used as public markers of presence on the wall.

The current wall is a live preview. The ownership engine, claim flow, payment rails, and launch protections are still being completed before full launch.
