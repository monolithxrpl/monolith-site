Digital land on the XRPL. One tile. One mark. Forever.
MONOLITH is a permanent on-chain grid, like land you can own on the XRP Ledger. Pick a coordinate and claim it.

  • Your wallet claim is written to the XRPL and timestamped.
  • Your mark is public, verifiable, and can’t be erased.
  • Years from now, people can prove who showed up early.
